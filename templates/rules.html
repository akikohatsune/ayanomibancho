COMMUNITY GUIDELINES & FAIR PLAY

Server Rules & Guidelines

To cultivate a respectful, healthy, and competitive osu! environment, all players participating on {{SERVER_NAME}} must strictly adhere to the following rules.

Rule 01
Respect & Community Conduct
Treat everyone with dignity. Harassment, hate speech, racism, regional slurs, abuse, and toxic behavior are strictly forbidden across public channels (#osu, #announce) and direct messages. Spamming, external server advertising, phishing links, and malicious exploits will result in immediate silence or restriction.
Rule 02
Fair Play & Zero Tolerance for Cheating
All forms of gameplay manipulation or automated cheating are strictly forbidden. This includes timewarp, unauthorized Relax hacks (only the official server !rx mode is permitted), replay submitters, auto-aim, cursor dance macros, keyboard macro scripts, and memory manipulation tools. Infringing accounts will face a permanent account ban and hardware ID (HWID) blacklist without warning.
Rule 03
One Account Per Player
Each individual is entitled to strictly one account. Creating secondary accounts (multi-accounting), account sharing, boosting, PP farming services, and buying or selling accounts are prohibited. Automated client hardware checks will reject duplicate registrations on the same machine.
Rule 04
Profile & Media Standards
Avatars, user bios, and display names must remain appropriate for all audiences. Content containing sexually explicit media (18+ NSFW), severe gore, hate symbols, or illegal material is strictly forbidden. Impersonating staff, developers, or official bots will result in immediate suspension.
Rule 05
Responsible Bug Disclosure & Staff Authority
If you discover a glitch, exploit, or security vulnerability, you must report it promptly to administration through the Staff & Credits page rather than exploiting it for personal gain. Administration decisions regarding server stability and integrity are final.
Important Notice: By connecting to and playing on {{SERVER_NAME}}, you acknowledge and agree to comply with all server rules and guidelines. Have fun and enjoy climbing the leaderboards!
