Method 1: Desktop Shortcut
Recommended
1
Locate or create your osu! shortcut

Navigate to your osu! installation directory (usually %LOCALAPPDATA%\osu!). Right click osu!.exe and select Send to → Desktop (create shortcut).

2
Add the -devserver flag to Properties

Right click the new shortcut, select Properties, and go to the Shortcut tab. In the Target field, add a space followed by -devserver {{SERVER_DOMAIN}} at the very end:

"C:\Users\YourUser\AppData\Local\osu!\osu!.exe" -devserver {{SERVER_DOMAIN}}
3
Launch and Log In!

Double click the shortcut to launch osu!. When the in-game login screen appears, simply enter your username and password. If your account is not yet registered, the server will automatically create and authenticate your account right away!

Method 2: One-Line Terminal Command
Quick Launch

Press Win + R, type powershell, and run this command:

Start-Process "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\osu!\osu!.exe" -ArgumentList "-devserver {{SERVER_DOMAIN}}"
SSL Certificate Setup (If Required)
Download CA Certificate (.crt)

If your osu! client warns about SSL or fails to connect over HTTPS, install our Root Certificate into Windows:

Option A: Manual Installation
  1. Click the Download CA Certificate button above.
  2. Double-click the downloaded ayanomi_ca.crt file.
  3. Click Install Certificate... → choose Current User.
  4. Select Place all certificates in the following store.
  5. Browse and select Trusted Root Certification Authorities.
  6. Click Next → Finish.
Option B: PowerShell One-Liner

Run PowerShell as Administrator after downloading:

Import-Certificate -FilePath "$HOME\Downloads\ayanomi_ca.crt" -CertStoreLocation Cert:\CurrentUser\Root
How to Switch Back to Official osu!

Because {{SERVER_NAME}} connects via the non-invasive -devserver flag, no client files are modified. Whenever you want to play on the official Bancho servers, simply launch osu!.exe normally without the -devserver argument!

Frequently Asked Questions
Can I download beatmaps in-game with osu!Direct?
Yes! osu!Direct is fully enabled and unlimited for all players on {{SERVER_NAME}}. You can search and download beatmaps directly from the in-game menus.
How does Relax (RX) ranking work?
Scores achieved with the Relax mod (RX) are automatically calculated and recorded in the Relax leaderboards for Standard, Taiko, and Catch modes. Check out the Relax Leaderboard!
Game says "Attempting to connect..." repeatedly?
Make sure the server is online and you're launching osu! with -devserver {{SERVER_DOMAIN}}. Also ensure your Windows Firewall allows connections on ports 80, 443, and 5000-5002.
How do I change my avatar and country flag?
Log in to your account on this website, then navigate to your Player Profile page to customize your avatar, bio, and country flag!
